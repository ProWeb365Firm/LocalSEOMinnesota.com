Shared Infrastructure

Why it matters more across a network

Location pages, store locators, and map integrations all run on the same build. What slows one down impacts the others. That's because your location pages all share it. It's the difference multi-location brands underestimate. Performance problems don't stay local. They multiply by however many pages run on the same build.

A single slow page costs you one visitor. A slow template costs you every location at once.

We size hosting to the page count you'll have in two years, not the one you have today.
