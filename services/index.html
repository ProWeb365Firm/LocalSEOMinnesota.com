Service 01

Listing and citation management

Your name, address, and phone need to match everywhere: your Google profile, Apple Maps, Bing, the data aggregators, and the industry directories relevant to your sector.

When those disagree, search engines lose confidence in your data and visibility lowers. Worse, the errors regenerate. Fix a directory without correcting the source it pulls from and it reverts next quarter.

We start by writing down the right record for every location, followed by improving upstream first and downstream after. Next, we re-check at 30, 90, and 180 days, because that's when the reverts show up.

Service 02

Google Business Profiles at scale

Managing two profiles is a task. Managing forty is a system.

We create location groups with proper permissions, so local managers can update hours and post photos while brand-level fields stay consistent. Every profile gets claimed under your business account.

Service 03

Location pages that rank separately

If two of these pages would still read correctly after swapping the city name, Google likely treats them as duplicates and shows only one.

We rebuild them around their differences. The people who work there, hours including seasonal changes, services that location offers, its own photos plus reviews, and local detail a real customer would recognize are all vital.

Where a location can't support a real page yet, we'll say so and keep it in the locator instead. Ten strong pages beat forty thin ones every time.

Service 04

Store locators search engines can actually read

Most store locators load these after the page does, through a map widget. Search engines often see an empty page.

That means your locator ranks for nothing and passes no strength down to your location pages, leaving each one to fend for itself.

It is rebuilt so the full list of locations is in the page itself, grouped by region, with real links. This includes the map sitting on top as a convenience rather than as the only way in. On a large network this is frequently the single highest-return fix available.

That last number is where growth appears. Multiple locations with identical traffic can be in completely different health.
