Quality Assurance

The check that catches what many miss

Contact forms, actually tested

Every month, we test the contact forms on a rotating sample of your location pages and confirm the messages arrive at the correct branch. A form routing to a manager who left eight months ago looks healthy from the outside. Even if the page loads, forms submit, thank-you's appear, enquiries go nowhere.

Site and profile, kept in step

We also check that every location page still matches its Google profile. Drift between the two is the most common cause of visibility loss we see. This happens quietly, one uncoordinated edit at a time.
