What changes the number

Start with the package matching your city count. These five variables help determine whether your scope needs anything beyond our published plan.

How many locations.

The main driver, though not linearly. The tenth location costs less to add than the second, because the system is already built.

What condition your data is in.

A brand with clean, consistent listings needs maintenance. Those with fifteen years of mergers, moves, and duplicate profiles need cleanup first.

How many location pages you need.

Pages you can genuinely fill with local detail, not a page per pin on a map.

Whether your locator needs rebuilding.

A one-time project on most networks, and often the highest-return item on the list.

How much your team can handle.

Brands whose local managers will maintain hours and photos need less from us than ones where everything routes through a single person.

How we charge

A clear, predictable approach designed for regional operators, keeping management costs scalable as your footprint expands.

No Arbitrary Volume Metrics

We don't charge per listing submitted. That model rewards volume, and adding your business to hundreds of directories nobody uses doesn’t improve rankings.

Transparent Cleanup Pricing

Cleanup projects like duplicate removal, aggregator corrections, locator rebuilds are quoted separately. They are classified as one-time work with a defined end so you can see what is ongoing.

City-Based Package Pricing

Choose Basic for 1 city, Silver for 2 cities, or Gold for up to 4 cities, with the included services clearly defined.

One-Year Agreements

Every package has a one-year contract, with a 10% savings when billed annually.
