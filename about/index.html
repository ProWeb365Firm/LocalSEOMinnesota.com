Why Multi-Location Work

A different problem, not a repeated one

A single business competes with the shop down the street. A twelve-location brand competes with itself first, then everyone else. None of these failure modes appear in standard local SEO advice.

Duplicate profiles

Created accidentally by managers or automated systems, splitting your reviews and authority.

Reverting listing data

Fixes that disappear after 30 days because an aggregator overrode your manual update.

Templated location pages

Find-and-replace copy fails to rank because it offers zero unique local value.

Unreadable locators

Store locators built in JavaScript that search engines can't crawl or properly index.

Gatekept operations

A corporate structure deciding who's allowed to change the hours.
